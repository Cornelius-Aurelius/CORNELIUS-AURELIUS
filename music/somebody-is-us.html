Verse 1

On a wall beside the station, a child had drawn the sun, above a hundred different faces with their hands joined into one. I stood there with a crowd of strangers, all saying, “Someone ought to try to make the real world look that hopeful.” Then I heard the words I’d said inside.

Pre-Chorus

We keep waiting for the fearless, for some hero to appear. But tomorrow isn't somewhere else— tomorrow starts right here.

Chorus

Somebody is us. The hands, the hearts, the hope we're waiting for. Somebody is us. Love doesn't live behind another door. If we dream of something better, we can't leave it up to luck. We keep waiting on somebody— Somebody is us.

Verse 2

A tired Man beneath an awning saw a stranger in the cold, took the only coat he carried and put it round his shoulders. No bells rang out above them. No one carved his name in stone. But for one small human moment, someone knew they weren't alone.

Pre-Chorus 2

Maybe changing all creation is too much for one to do. But the world becomes a little more like whatever passes through you.

Chorus

Somebody is us. The hands, the hearts, the hope we're waiting for. Somebody is us. Love doesn't live behind another door. If we dream of something better, we can't leave it up to luck. We keep waiting on somebody— Somebody is us.

Bridge

I used to ask for someone braver, someone stronger than I am. Someone born to move the mountains, someone with a greater plan. Then one morning in the mirror there was nowhere left to run. And I finally heard the question: “What if you're the someone?” Time won't keep our good intentions if they never leave our mouths. History remembers footsteps, not the fingers of the crowd. And love is more than wishing. Love is choosing. Love is done. No single Man can heal the whole world— but the healing starts with one.

Breakdown

One hand. One heart. One voice saying, “I'll begin.” Then another. Then another. Till the stranger becomes kin.

Final Chorus

SOMEBODY IS US! The hands, the hearts, the hope we've waited for. SOMEBODY IS US! We don't leave love outside another door. If we dream of something better, then the dreaming isn't enough. No more waiting on somebody— SOMEBODY IS US. Let the hungry find a table. Let the frightened find a friend. Let forgiveness break the circle someone thought would never end. Let every child inherit more than what was handed us. If somebody must build a better world— SOMEBODY IS US. Gospel Refrain Somebody is us. (We are the ones.) Somebody is us. (Let the love come.) One heart can wake another heart, one light can wake another light. Till millions who were waiting stand together and decide— Somebody is us.

Outro

Maybe no one person saves the world. Maybe that's the point. Maybe the better world begins whenever one heart makes a choice. And if we give the children more love than fear ever gave to us, one day the dream will feel so ordinary they'll never know it was a dream.
