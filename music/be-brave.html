Be Brave

By Cornelius Aurelius

Listen on SUNO ↗Read lyrics ↓

Lyrics

Verse 1

The mirror keeps no secrets— it only shows the man you made. Not the man you say you are, but the one your choices name. A little boy is watching how you carry being strong, how you speak when you are angry, what you do when you are wrong. And the woman standing near you should never learn to fear the hands that promised love to her, or the voice she once held dear.

Pre-Chorus

Strength is not a harder heart. It isn't hiding every scar. Sometimes strength is stopping pain from travelling where you are.

Chorus

Be brave. Be brave enough to dream. Be brave. Be better than you've been. Be brave enough to carry the burden that you bear without laying it on someone who was never meant to wear it. Be brave. When truth is hard to face. Be brave. When love demands a change. If you dream of a better world, don't wait for one to come. Be brave enough to become one.

Verse 2

A woman shouldn't have to wonder which man walks through the door. A child should never read your footsteps like they've heard that storm before. You can blame the men before you. You can say, “That's how I learned.” But pain is not an inheritance another heart must earn. And when another man calls cruelty the proof that he is strong, don't laugh because it's easier. Be brave enough to say he's wrong.

Pre-Chorus

You can carry what they gave you without giving it away. You can be the place the hurting finally ends today.

Chorus

Be brave. Be brave enough to dream. Be brave. Be better than you've been. Be brave enough to carry the burden that you bear without turning love to fear for the people in your care. Be brave. When pride says, “Never bend.” Be brave. Say, “I was wrong”—and mean it then. If you dream of a better world, let the better world begin with you.

Bridge

Now stand before the mirror. No crowd. No blame. No place to hide. Look that man straight in the eyes and ask what he leaves behind. If your anger made her smaller— own it. If your words became her fear— own it. If a child learned how to tremble when your footsteps came too near— own it. Don't call shame accountability. Don't turn guilt into escape. The bravest thing a man can do is face the hurt he helped create. Say: I did it. I was wrong. I will listen. I will change. Then prove those words tomorrow when the easy promise fades. Because sorry isn't courage if the hurting stays the same. And love is not what you promise. Love is what becomes safe.

Final Chorus

BE BRAVE. Be brave enough to dream of boys who grow to men who never need to prove their strength by frightening someone else again. BE BRAVE. For every woman who deserves to walk through her own door and know the man who says he loves her will make her fear no more. BE BRAVE. For every little child who deserves to understand that love is never something they should fear inside a man. BE BRAVE. When your brother needs the truth. BE BRAVE. And help him find it too. If we dream of a better world, then dreaming isn't enough. Look into the mirror. Face the man you've become. And if tomorrow needs a better man— be brave enough to be one.

Outro

Maybe the better world begins without thunder from above. Maybe it begins the day a man decides that strength is love. And somewhere years from now, a child will grow up unafraid— because one man carried his pain and was brave enough not to give it away.
