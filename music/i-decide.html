I Decide

By Cornelius Aurelius

Listen on SUNO ↗Listen on YouTube ↗Read lyrics ↓

Lyrics

Verse 1

They taught me to knock softly on doors with my name on them, to wait outside and call it grace, then smile and ask again. I learned to lower my laughter when the room got too still, turn “I want” into “maybe,” turn “I can” into “if you will.” I spent years reading faces, trying not to take too much— till I finally asked myself: Who taught me I needed their permission to become who I already was?

Pre-Chorus

I don't need another green light. I don't need another sign. This heart, this voice, this one life— they're mine.

Chorus

Who tells me who I'm gonna be? I decide. Who tells me how high I can reach? I decide. I won't make myself smaller just to fit somebody's life. If the door has got my name on it, I'm walking through tonight. What I want. What I'm worth. Where I'm going with this life— I decide.

Verse 2

I've cried in bathroom mirrors, then walked back in composed. I've smiled while being talked across by someone who knew less than I know. I've stayed where love meant shrinking. I've said sorry just for breathing. Then one night I turned a handle and learned that leaving can be freedom. Now the scars don't make me weaker. They remind me I survived. Every time somebody dimmed me, something brighter stayed alive.

Pre-Chorus 2

I don't need to be fearless to finally be brave. Sometimes courage is a shaking hand that turns the handle anyway.

Chorus

Who tells me who I'm gonna be? I decide. Who tells me what I'm allowed to dream? I decide. I won't make myself smaller. I won't apologize for my light. If the door has got my name on it, I'm walking through tonight. What I love. What I leave. What I'm willing to survive— I decide.

Bridge

I used to think confidence meant never being scared. I was wrong. Confidence is hearing “no” and refusing to turn it into your name. It's losing the room without losing yourself. It's walking away from any love that asks you to disappear. If my voice shakes— let it shake. If my knees shake— let them shake. I can be terrified and still take one more step. I can be soft and still be powerful. I can fall and still get up. And I do not need to become less to be loved by anyone. So no— I am not too loud. I am not too much. I am not too ambitious. I am not too late. I am done measuring my life with somebody else's tape.

Final Chorus

WHO TELLS ME WHO I'M GONNA BE? I DECIDE! WHO TELLS ME HOW HIGH I CAN REACH? I DECIDE! I won't make myself smaller. I won't stand politely outside. If the door has got my name on it— I'M WALKING THROUGH TONIGHT. What I want. What I'm worth. What I do with this one life— I DECIDE. But if you're standing at a doorway with that same fear in your eyes, put your hand upon the handle. You don't need to ask them twice. Come beside me. Bring your courage. Bring the dream you kept inside. This was never only my door. WE DECIDE. Final Lift WE DECIDE! How we rise. WE DECIDE! How we live. WE DECIDE! What we're worth and what we'll never again give up to fit. No permission. No apology. No more waiting outside. Every woman coming after us— WE HOLD THE DOOR OPEN WIDE.

Outro

They taught me to knock softly on doors with my name on them. Funny. I don't knock anymore. I walk through. And when I see another woman waiting where I used to wait— I hold the door. Because my power isn't only choosing my own life. It's making sure she knows she can choose hers. I decide. We decide.
