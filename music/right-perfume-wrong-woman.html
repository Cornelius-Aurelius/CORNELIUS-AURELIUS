Right Perfume, Wrong Woman

By Cornelius Aurelius

Lyrics

Verse 1 (country swing, with a wink)

Bartender saw me coming, poured it slow, Said, "Son, you know where this one goes." I said, "Sure do," and I left him a twenty, Fixing my collar, following my nose.

Pre-Chorus

Eyes that say stay, lips that say maybe, And a phone that lights up at one a.m.

Chorus (snaps to driving pop-rock)

Right perfume, wrong woman, Jasmine, smoke, and a bad idea in heels. Every sign says "Turn around," I turn the radio up and spin the wheels. I know better, still I'm going, Right perfume, wrong woman.

Verse 2

She's got me saved as "Maybe" in her phone, Right below the pilot, right above the plumber. I've been waiting on a call since June, And it's the coldest, longest summer.

Pre-Chorus 2

She says "Come over," means it for an hour, Says "Go home," and means that too.

Chorus 2

Right perfume, wrong woman, Jasmine, smoke, and a bad idea in heels. Every friend I've got says "Turn around," I say, "I hear you," and I spin the wheels. I know better, still I'm going, Right perfume, wrong woman.

Bridge (drops to bass, pedal steel and a hushed voice)

She never lied. She said it plain: "I'm not the staying kind." I heard a dare, I heard a game, I heard what I went looking to find. So I rolled the window down And let the night take her off my collar.

Final Chorus (key change up a whole step)

Right perfume, wrong woman, Jasmine, smoke, and a bad idea in heels. Every sign says "Turn around," So I turn the radio down, and I turn the wheel. I know better — and I'm going home. Right perfume, wrong woman.

Outro (spoken, then one horn stab)

Right perfume… Wrong woman. Lord, that was a perfume.
