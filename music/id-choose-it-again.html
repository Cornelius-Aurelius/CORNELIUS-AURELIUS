I’d Choose It Again

Lyrics

Female version

Verse 1

The doctor left the room this morning, left the daylight on the floor. Funny how a woman sees things clearly when she knows there’s not much more. I can hear the clock beside me. I can feel the evening close. But I’m not asking time for mercy. I’ve had more life than most will know. They said, “Slow down, you’re crazy.” They said, “Girl, you’ll lose it all.” Maybe I was born believing there was something past the wall.

Pre-Chorus

I took roads they called impossible. I knocked on doors marked “No.” Sometimes I found a paradise. Sometimes I found out why they wrote it so.

Chorus

Call me crazy. Call me stupid. Call me anything you please. Call me a fool for chasing freedom when I could have lived with ease. I have scars I wouldn’t ask for. I have nights I’d change somehow. But if you gave me back the years and let me choose them now— I’d take the love. I’d take the pain. I’d take the sun. I’d take the rain. Knowing where the road would end, I’d choose it all again.

Verse 2

I loved some people badly. I let some good ones walk away. There were words I should have swallowed and some I should have found the strength to say. I was told to be more careful, not to want so much from life. To be grateful, quiet, sensible, to fit inside somebody else’s lines. But I wanted all the colours. I wanted every open door. And every time they said, “Be reasonable,” I heard something in me asking for more.

Pre-Chorus 2

Yes, regret has sat beside me. Yes, I’ve paid for what I’ve done. But every wrong turn left me somewhere. Every darkness taught me sun.

Chorus

Call me crazy. Call me stupid. Call me anything you please. Call me a fool for choosing freedom over living on my knees. I have scars I wouldn’t ask for. I have tears I’d still allow. Because the woman lying here tonight is the woman those choices made somehow. I’d take the love. I’d take the pain. I’d lose my heart and love again. Knowing every broken end, I’d choose it all again.

Bridge

Come closer. There’s one thing I need you to know. Don’t spend your whole life becoming what everybody says you owe. They called me crazy when I walked away from what was killing me. They called me stupid when I trusted what I couldn’t prove. They called me foolish every time I loved with everything I had. Funny— those were the moments that made this life worth losing. So yes— I have regrets. Of course I do. I regret the hands I didn’t hold. The apologies I made too late. The days I traded for tomorrow when tomorrow could have waited. I regret the times I made myself smaller just to make somebody stay. But I do not regret becoming the woman I fought to be today. And if there’s one thing death has taught me: a life can be imperfect and still be a masterpiece.

Final Chorus

So call me crazy! I was crazy enough to dream. Call me stupid! I believed in things unseen. Call me a fool! I loved when love could leave. And if that makes me foolish— what a blessed fool I’ve been. I took the laughter. I took the blame. I took the glory. I took the shame. I broke. I healed. I lost. I changed. And I would do it all the same. Not because I got it perfect. Not because I always won. But because when my time is over, I will know I truly lived one. Give me every road behind me. Give me every scar and friend. Give me every beautiful mistake— I’d choose it all again.

Outro

The room is getting quieter now. The evening’s almost through. And for once I don’t need another tomorrow. I had today. I had yesterday. I had love. I had a life that never fit inside the lines. And if somewhere there’s a question asking whether I would change it in the end— I already know the answer. I’d choose it again. Male version
