Deal Me In Again

By Cornelius Aurelius

Listen on YouTube ↗Read lyrics ↓

Lyrics

Verse 1

Lost the ranch on a pair of queens, Lost my truck to a Tulsa fog, Lost my girl and a couple of teeth, And a bet on a three-legged dog.

Pre-Chorus

But every sunrise is a dealer, friend, Cool as ice, and he asks me, "You in?"

Chorus

So deal me in again, deal me in, Scoot my chair back up to the game. Luck can come and go as she pleases, But I'm the one who stays. Deal me in again.

Verse 2

Pushed my chair back, left the room, Sat in the cab at the edge of town, Counted up what a man can lose, Then laughed, and wiped my face, and turned around.

Pre-Chorus 2

'Cause a sunrise never holds a grudge, friend, He never mentions the hand I lost.

Chorus 2

So deal me in again, deal me in, Scoot my chair back up to the game. Luck's been gone since Tulsa, sure enough, But I'm the one who stays. Deal me in again.

Bridge (drops to bass, brushes and a hushed voice)

My hands still shake when the cards come down. Any man who says they don't, he's lying. But there's a kid in the doorway taking notes On how a man falls, and how he gets back up.

Final Chorus (key change up a whole step)

So deal me in again, deal us in, Pull up a chair, son, one more for the game. Luck can come and go as she pleases, But we're the ones who stay. Deal us in again.

Outro (spoken, then one horn stab)

Hands still shake, son. Deal 'em anyway.
