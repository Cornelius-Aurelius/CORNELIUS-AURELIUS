Lyrics

Verse 1

The mirror held my face this morning, but I saw more than only mine— all the hands that built the fences, all the hearts behind the lines. And somewhere underneath the anger, under everything we fear, I heard one question rise between us: Who would we be without each other here?

Pre-Chorus

Maybe hope is not a promise falling from some distant blue. Maybe hope begins the moment I can see myself in you.

Chorus

Dream it true, dream it true, let tomorrow start with me and you. When I see myself within your eyes, I can't turn my back on you. We don't have to wait on heaven— love has work for us to do. Take the world we keep on wishing for and dream it true.

Verse 2

An old Man at the station held his umbrella wide for a mother and her little boy with nowhere dry to hide. No one cheered, no cameras found it, time just carried on its way— but I swear the world grew kinder for one minute in that rain.

Pre-Chorus 2

If one hand can change a moment, one moment can change a mind, maybe love begins that quietly then keeps moving down the line.

Chorus

Dream it true, dream it true, let tomorrow start with me and you. When I see myself within your eyes, I can't turn my back on you. We don't have to wait on heaven— love has work for us to do. Take the world we keep on wishing for and dream it true.

Bridge

I kept waiting for a better Man, for a hero, for a sign— then the mirror spoke my name and asked, “What will you do with your time?” I can't heal every sorrow. I can't make the whole world new. But I can leave more love behind me than the fear I ever knew. And if you do the same beside me, and another does it too, maybe all the world is waiting for what ordinary hearts can do.

Final Chorus

Dream it true—dream it true! Let tomorrow start with what we do. Every hand can be a shelter, every heart can make room too. We don't have to wait on heaven— love has work for us to do. Let the frightened find their courage, let the lonely make it through. For every child who's still believing, for every soul still reaching through, take the world we've only dreamed of— and dream it true. Dream it true! Till no one has to stand alone. Till every stranger can come home. Till mercy speaks louder than fear ever knew— Dream it true.

Outro

And one day may a child look in the mirror and never wish the world were new. May the dream we carried for them be the world they wake up to.
