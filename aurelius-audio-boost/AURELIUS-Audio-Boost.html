Compatibility and playback

Can it boost YouTube or audio from other apps?

The browser edition adjusts playback inside this player. YouTube, streaming tabs and audio from other apps are outside its scope.

Which files and devices can I use?

Use a browser with Web Audio support on your phone, tablet or computer. MP3 and WAV are good starting points. The formats you can play depend on your browser. With a supported video file, the player plays its audio track.

What does the download do?

It saves a standalone HTML file. On a device that lets you open local HTML in a browser, you can use the player offline. Mobile devices that preview downloaded HTML can use the online player above.

Is there a native Android or iPhone app?

The browser edition is available now. Native downloads are pending verification.
