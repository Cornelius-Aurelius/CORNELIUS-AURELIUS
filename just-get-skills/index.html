How Skill-Links Work

Specific before generic.

1. Resolve the source and target

Match everyday wording and aliases to the canonical skill universe.

2. Decompose both

Break them into observable subskills, abilities and mechanisms.

3. Search downward

Prefer a specific unit such as single-leg dynamic balance over broad “balance” where justified.

4. Reject weak analogies

Focus, discipline or teamwork alone are not enough.

5. Explain the causal link

Source behaviour → shared subskill → target behaviour → useful head start.

6. Keep limits visible

A Skill-Link does not replace target-specific technique, rules or knowledge.
