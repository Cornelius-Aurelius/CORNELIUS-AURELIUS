How JGS decides

Specific before generic.

1. Resolve the pair

Understand the source and target skill.

2. Decompose both

Break each into concrete, observable subskills.

3. Find the smallest overlap

Prefer single-leg dynamic balance over generic balance when justified.

4. Reject weak links

Focus, discipline and teamwork alone are not enough.

5. Explain the transfer

Source behaviour → exact reusable unit → target behaviour.

6. Keep the limits visible

Transferable foundations do not replace target-specific learning.
