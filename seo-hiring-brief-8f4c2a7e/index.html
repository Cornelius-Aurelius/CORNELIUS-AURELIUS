How I work

I prefer a repeatable process over SEO theatre.

My default is to move from evidence to implementation and then measure what actually happened.

01

Research

Intent, competitors, SERPs, customer language and available data.

02

Diagnose

Crawlability, indexation, architecture, content, metadata, schema and UX friction.

03

Prioritise

Impact, effort, commercial relevance and confidence before changing things.

04

Implement

I like actually shipping fixes instead of leaving every useful idea inside an audit.

05

Measure & improve

Search Console, analytics, crawls, page tests and business outcomes where data is available.

Working fit

I would rather tell you the truth before you hire me.

I am strongest when the job rewards focused work, curiosity, technical problem solving, implementation and clear written communication.

Strong fit

  • Remote, focused SEO work with clear ownership
  • Technical and on-page investigation
  • E-commerce and content-led SEO
  • Search Console, analytics and indexing work
  • Building, testing and fixing websites
  • Written documentation and asynchronous communication
  • Teams that value experimentation and continued learning

Probably not the right fit

  • Phone-heavy sales or cold-calling roles
  • Constant meetings as the main way work gets done
  • Account-management-heavy agency roles
  • Jobs where appearing busy matters more than shipping useful work
  • Roles expecting me to pretend I have experience I do not have

Lighthouse is technical proof, not ranking proof.

A high score shows that a page passed the checks Lighthouse covered in that run. It does not prove organic traffic, rankings, leads or revenue.

Search and AI results can vary.

I test how properties appear in search and AI-assisted discovery, but I do not present one screenshot as a permanent ranking. Dates, prompts, platforms, locations and repeatability matter.
